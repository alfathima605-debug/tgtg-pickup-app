Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Overview

Pickup App ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

All order data, including order details, codes, and pickup information, is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any server.

Data Storage

All application data is stored locally on your device using browser/local storage. This data:

  • Is never transmitted to external servers
  • Is not accessible by us or any third party
  • Remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app

Internet Usage

This app uses internet access solely to load its web content. No user data, analytics, or tracking information is transmitted.

Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app's Help & Support section.
